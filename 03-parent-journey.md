# 03 — Parent/guardian journey

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

> **Status:** Review draft
> **Principle:** The parent supports; the student remains the decision owner.

## Parent job

> “Hjälp mig förstå alternativen och ha ett bättre samtal utan att lägga min oro eller mina ambitioner på mitt barn.”

Parents may have essential context about travel, cost, wellbeing and logistics. They must not receive covert monitoring, hidden inferred traits, or control of recommendations.

## Entry modes

### 1. Public parent guide — no account

- Plain explanation of Swedish gymnasium choices and key terms.
- Conversation prompts.
- Links to official sources.
- No access to a student.

### 2. Student-invited conversation — MVP

1. Student chooses **“Bjud in till samtal.”**
2. Student sees exactly what will be shared.
3. Student chooses scopes:
   - saved shortlist;
   - comparison card;
   - possible path;
   - selected questions;
   - never full discovery history by default.
4. Link expires in 7 days by default and can be shortened/revoked.
5. Parent sees “Det här har eleven valt att dela.”
6. Parent may add a visible comment or question; cannot edit student content.
7. Student can accept, ignore or remove comments.

### 3. Linked parent account — post-pilot candidate

Only if evidence shows a persistent need. Must remain student-visible and revocable, subject to safeguarding/legal exceptions defined outside ordinary product flow.

## Journey

| Stage | Parent need | Experience | Agency safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understand role | “How should I help?” | Start with “Stötta utan att styra” and three do/don’t examples. | Product never calls the parent “decision maker.” |
| Understand terms | “Behörig versus likely to enter?” | Short term cards with source/date. | No admission probability. |
| Receive invitation | “What am I allowed to see?” | Exact scope and expiry before opening. | No inference beyond shared payload. |
| Review options | “What are the trade-offs?” | Same separated dimensions as student; parent lens adds logistics questions. | No hidden parent-only ranking. |
| Discuss | “How do I ask without pressure?” | Prompt cards: ask, listen, check understanding. | Avoid prestige/salary-first defaults. |
| Add context | “Travel could be hard.” | Add a visible note: `Fråga att prata om`. | Parent cannot silently change feasibility. |
| End | “What happens to my access?” | Expiry/revocation shown; no data retained beyond necessity. | Student gets a clear access history. |

## Parent home copy

```text
Du är här för att stötta
Det är eleven som väljer och äger sin väg.

Du kan:
• läsa det eleven har delat
• ställa frågor som eleven ser
• kontrollera källor tillsammans

Du kan inte:
• se elevens privata svar
• ändra elevens väg
• få en hemlig bedömning
```

## Conversation cards

### Helpful prompts

- “Vad känns intressant i det här alternativet?”
- “Vad känns fortfarande oklart?”
- “Vilken skillnad mellan alternativen spelar störst roll för dig?”
- “Vilken uppgift behöver vi kontrollera?”
- “Vill du att jag hjälper dig, eller vill du bara att jag lyssnar?”
- “Vad vill du fråga din SYV?”

### Pressure warning

If the parent selects ranking/salary repeatedly, the UI can neutrally add:

> Lön och tidigare antagningspoäng är delar av bilden. Fråga också om vardag, innehåll, stöd, resväg och vad eleven vill lära sig.

This is static product guidance, not a judgement of the parent.

## Conflict handling

A parent and student may disagree. MINVÄG does not mediate family conflict or choose a side. It can:

- place both visible statements beside the option;
- separate factual questions from preferences;
- propose: “Spara båda frågorna till samtalet med SYV”; and
- provide a safety/support route if a student reports feeling unsafe.

It must not expose the student’s private notes to resolve disagreement.

## Logistics without surveillance

- Travel starts from a coarse area or selected transit stop, not exact home address.
- Cost content comes from official sources and relevant dates.
- Parent can suggest a constraint; the student decides whether it becomes a profile preference.
- No location history, background tracking or parent geofencing.

## Notifications

Default: none. Optional notifications must be transactional and user-requested, e.g. invitation expiry. Never send “Your child has not completed their next step.”

## Privacy model

- Parent invitation token is random, single-purpose, expiring, revocable and not included in analytics URLs.
- Require additional verification before displaying sensitive shared data; exact design depends on pilot risk review.
- The student can view last access and revoke immediately.
- Parent comments have a clear retention rule and are deleted with the share/account.
- No parent receives raw prompts, model transcripts, grades or exact location unless the student explicitly adds them to the brief—and grades should remain excluded by default.

## Parent success criteria (Gate 8 inputs)

- Parent can explain their role and access scope.
- Student correctly predicts what parent sees.
- Parent identifies one supportive question and one fact to verify.
- Student reports decision ownership did not decrease.
- No material increase in pressure in before/after student interviews.
- Sharing/revocation succeeds with assistive technology.

## Explicit exclusions

- Parent dashboard monitoring all student activity.
- Parent-selected career or programme lock.
- Parent control of recommendation factors.
- Hidden “risk”, “potential”, personality or motivation score.
- Push alerts about grades, browsing or inactivity.
- Family messaging/social feed.
- Paid parent advantage or premium ranking.
