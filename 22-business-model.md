# 22 — Business model

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

> **Status:** Hypotheses, not validated pricing or demand
> **Commercial principle:** revenue can never change recommendation order, visibility, evidence or student access to core guidance

## 1. Beneficiary, user and buyer

| Role | Value hypothesis | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Earlier clarity, realistic branches and one useful next action | Desk evidence supports broad need; product value unvalidated. |
| Parent/guardian | Lower-pressure, better-informed conversation | Unvalidated. |
| SYV | Better-prepared student conversations and traceable facts | Unvalidated; close B2B competitors exist. |
| School/municipality | Consistent early career-learning support, aggregate programme insight and trusted data operations without replacing SYV | Unvalidated buyer/willingness/procurement. |
| Authority/data owner | More visible correct attribution and structured correction route | Relationship hypothesis. |

The person receiving a recommendation is not the inventory. MINVÄG does not sell student profiles, leads, attention or ranking.

## 2. Proposed model

### Student core — free

Free access to:

- anonymous explore;
- living profile and eligibility guidance;
- programme/school/career exploration;
- recommendations with explanations;
- My Path, compare and next action;
- source/provenance;
- basic parent conversation and SYV brief;
- privacy/export/delete.

No paywall around safety, eligibility, sources, correction or alternatives.

### Institutional subscription — hypothesis

School/municipality pays for a bounded service package:

- organisation-managed SYV access to student-created briefs;
- approved onboarding/training/materials;
- aggregate minimum-cell usage/comprehension/service quality (no individual surveillance);
- source/data quality support and correction workflow;
- tenant/security/privacy administration;
- pilot/evaluation support;
- later, approved system integration only if necessary and lawful.

Contract is annual per organisation/cohort/service tier only after pricing discovery. **No prices are asserted in this plan.** Public competitor institutional prices were largely undisclosed.

### Optional professional services

- implementation/accessibility/privacy readiness workshops;
- local source coverage assessment;
- SYV facilitation/training;
- ethical pilot/evaluation design.

Services must not create authority over the student or bespoke ranking manipulation.

## 3. Rejected revenue

- pay-to-rank or sponsored recommendations;
- paid school visibility inside neutral results;
- selling student contact/lead data;
- behavioural or profiling-based advertising;
- employer recruitment access to minors;
- marketplace commission for placements/applications;
- premium eligibility/provenance or a “better future” tier;
- sale/reuse of student data to train models;
- affiliate payments that change recommendation/action links.

If any sponsorship/funding acknowledgement exists, it is clearly labelled outside the recommendation system and has no targeting/ranking data path.

## 4. Value proposition by buyer

### Head of school / education administration

- complements—not replaces—qualified guidance;
- helps start career learning in Grade 8;
- consistent source-visible student experience;
- supports constructive not-yet-eligible conversations;
- privacy/minor/accessibility architecture suitable for due diligence;
- aggregate evidence about whether students understood and acted, without a named “risk list.”

### SYV lead

- student-owned questions before meetings;
- exact source/rule lineage;
- correction and stale-data workflow;
- fewer basic clarification steps if the brief works;
- no hidden AI judgment.

### DPO/IT/security/procurement

- minimised/no-personnummer account design;
- EEA-first vendor and transfer documentation;
- role/share isolation and no advertising trackers;
- DPIA support, retention/deletion and audit evidence;
- WCAG/EN 301 549 evidence;
- transparent incident/subprocessor/SLA/exit terms.

## 5. Market context—not a revenue forecast

Official 2025/26 statistics report approximately 1.1 million compulsory-school students and 375,449 gymnasium students, including 37,011 on introduction programmes. [S09, S27](SOURCES.md#product-education-and-data-sources) Spring 2025 had 123,739 Grade 9 leavers, 19,700 lacking eligibility for a national programme. [S08](SOURCES.md#product-education-and-data-sources) These describe cohorts and equity context; they do **not** prove product demand, addressable contract value, price or adoption.

Build a bottom-up market model only after buyer discovery:

```text
eligible Swedish buyer organisations by segment
× evidenced procurement/willingness rate
× tested annual contract value
= serviceable annual contract hypothesis
```

Show low/base/high with assumptions and procurement lead times. Do not present a global education-market percentage as credible TAM.

## 6. Pricing research

Interview at least:

- 6 municipal education/SYV leaders across size/geography;
- 4 school groups/independent providers;
- 4 procurement/IT/DPO stakeholders;
- 6 practising SYVs (may overlap organisational interviews).

Test:

- current workflow/cost/problem priority;
- existing tools and switching/contract constraints;
- who owns budget and procurement threshold/calendar;
- value metric (cohort, school unit, municipality, active student, support tier);
- acceptable pilot and annual structures;
- required accessibility/security/privacy evidence;
- buyer response to no individual dashboard and no pay-to-rank;
- willingness to pay, using structured methods—not “Would you buy?” alone.

Record “no budget/no need/existing solution sufficient” evidence prominently.

## 7. Proposed packaging for research

| Package concept | Includes | Excludes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open student | Core student/parent/public SYV features | Organisation admin and SLA |
| School guidance | Organisation identities, share workflow, training, aggregate reports, standard support | Individual surveillance, custom ranking |
| Municipality coordination | Multiple school units, governance/source coverage, aggregate cross-unit reporting with minimum cells, enhanced support | Named cross-school profiles |
| Pilot/evaluation | Time-limited onboarding, research and outcome report | Automatic production renewal/dark pattern |

Do not set prices until costs and willingness are observed.

## 8. Cost model

Track per month and per activated student/organisation:

- product/design/engineering;
- Swedish domain content and SYV review;
- data ingestion/licensing/correction stewardship;
- cloud/database/storage/traffic;
- AI calls (should be small and removable);
- transactional email/security/monitoring vendors;
- support/onboarding/training;
- privacy/legal/security/accessibility audits;
- sales/procurement and contract management;
- incident/risk reserve.

Primary cost risk is likely trusted data/content/organisation support rather than model tokens. Validate.

## 9. Unit economics template

No invented values. Fill after pilot:

```text
Annual recurring contract revenue (ACV)
− direct cloud/data/vendor cost
− allocated onboarding/support/SYV-review cost
= contribution margin per contract

Customer acquisition cost
= attributable sales + procurement + pilot conversion effort
  ÷ new paying organisations

Payback
= CAC ÷ monthly contribution margin

Net retention
= opening recurring revenue + expansion − contraction − churn
  ÷ opening recurring revenue
```

Also track data incident/correction burden, source coverage cost per region and implementation hours; do not hide them in “R&D” when assessing sustainability.

## 10. Go-to-market hypothesis

1. Co-design with a small, diverse student advisory group and independent SYVs.
2. Partner with 2–3 pilot schools/municipalities that accept the no-surveillance/no-ranking principles.
3. Publish source coverage, safety/privacy/accessibility evidence and honest pilot outcomes.
4. Build referrals through SYV professional networks and municipal education leaders.
5. Use a standard due-diligence pack and predictable deployment to reduce bespoke sales work.
6. Convert only if student/SYV outcomes and operational economics meet gates.

Do not use school advertising packages or exclusive provider content to finance early growth.

## 11. Contracts and governance

- Controller/processor roles per deployment; no ownership claim over student data.
- Purpose/subprocessor/transfer/security/accessibility/SLA/incident/deletion/exit schedules.
- Customer cannot request a recommendation boost, hidden student score or data reuse.
- Standard correction/escalation responsibilities.
- Data portability and verified deletion at contract end.
- Public conflict-of-interest and recommendation-independence policy.
- Commercial staff have no write access to ranking/recommendation configuration.

## 12. Sustainability Gate 10

Pass only if:

- at least a defined set of representative buyers demonstrates budget authority and credible willingness through pilots/procurement steps, not compliments;
- student, parent and SYV value gates pass;
- contribution margin path includes real data stewardship, support, compliance and audit costs;
- sales/procurement cycle and implementation effort are financeable;
- no revenue depends on pay-to-rank, lead sale, ads, surveillance or data reuse;
- source/licence/vendor dependencies have viable alternatives;
- a funded 12–18 month operating plan with downside case and stop point is approved.

Until then the business model is a hypothesis, not a forecast.
