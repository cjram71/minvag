## SYV job

> “Hjälp eleven förbereda sina egna tankar och frågor och ge mig spårbar fakta, så att vårt samtal kan fokusera på vägledning, nyanser och beslutskompetens.”

Swedish official reviews document uneven/limited guidance and the need for broader career learning. [S02](SOURCES.md#product-education-and-data-sources) There were 2,830 practising SYVs, equal to 2,150 full-time posts, across the school system in 2025/26; that does not directly measure each target student’s access. [S10](SOURCES.md#product-education-and-data-sources)

## Two modes

### Public professional mode — no student data

- Explore rules, sources, source changes, programme structures and pathway claim evidence.
- Report an error or interpretation issue.
- Use synthetic case tools for professional review.

### Student handoff mode — MVP

Student creates and explicitly shares a brief. The SYV does not browse the student’s full account.

## Student-owned brief

```text
Inför mitt SYV-samtal
Skapad av eleven: 2 september 2026
Delning slutar: 9 september 2026

Jag utforskar just nu
• Teknikprogrammet — gillar problemlösning; osäker på studietakten
• El- och energiprogrammet — gillar praktiska uppgifter; vill förstå vägar vidare

Det jag vet / inte vet
• Behörighet: ser möjlig ut utifrån betyg jag själv fyllt i
• Antagningspoäng: historiska uppgifter saknas för ett alternativ
• Resväg: inte kontrollerad

Mina frågor
1. Vad skiljer vardagen i programmen?
2. Vilka lokala inriktningar erbjuds faktiskt nästa år?
3. Vilket nästa steg tycker du att jag ska undersöka?

MINVÄG har inte valt åt mig. Fakta och källor finns på sida 2.
```

By default, omit raw grades, full profile history, inferred fit and family comments.

## Journey

| Stage | SYV need | Experience | System boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Receive | Know who shared what and for how long | Student name/alias, scope, expiry, purpose | No search for unshared students. |
| Triage | Understand reason for conversation | Student questions and explicitly saved options first | No risk or motivation score. |
| Verify | Inspect rule/data basis | Claim, source, date, rule version, unknown/conflict | LLM text never substitutes for source. |
| Converse | Use time for reflective guidance | Printable/accessible brief and note prompts | MINVÄG does not listen to or transcribe meeting. |
| Correct | Flag wrong fact/interpretation | Structured report linked to claim/entity | Correction reviewed; no direct overwrite of authority. |
| Close | Return ownership to student | SYV may suggest a visible action; student accepts/edits | No hidden assignment or compliance monitoring. |

## SYV suggested action workflow

1. SYV selects from the same finite action catalogue or writes a short visible suggestion.
2. Student receives: “Din SYV föreslog detta.”
3. Student chooses `Lägg till`, `Ändra` or `Inte nu`.
4. No completion status is returned to the SYV in MVP unless the student explicitly shares a later brief.

## Organisation access

For a school-procured pilot:

- organisation-managed SYV identities;
- phishing-resistant MFA;
- roles scoped by school/cohort only where a lawful need exists;
- access is not granted merely because a person works at the same school;
- just-in-time access to a student share, not standing access to all profiles;
- access logs visible to security/privacy administrators and, where appropriate, the student;
- no bulk export of individual profiles;
- aggregated dashboard only after minimum-cell thresholds and pilot validation.

## Data correction flow

A SYV report contains:

- entity/claim ID;
- issue type: fact wrong, date stale, rule interpretation, wording misleading, source conflict, accessibility;
- proposed evidence link;
- severity: critical eligibility/safety, high pathway, normal editorial;
- reporter role and contact (not shown publicly);
- status and resolution audit.

Critical reports may suppress a claim and invoke a safe fallback while reviewed. Only authorised data stewards change source precedence or rules.

## Trust display for professionals

Every evaluation includes:

- rule version and valid period;
- input provenance (`student_self_reported`, not school-verified);
- source snapshot IDs;
- claims used and exclusions;
- calculation trace in plain Swedish;
- content generated by template versus AI;
- model/prompt version for AI explanation;
- conflict/staleness flags.

## What AI may and may not do

**May:** summarise an already calculated result, simplify source-grounded content, suggest questions from an approved taxonomy, detect missing information for the user to verify.

**May not:** decide eligibility, predict admission, diagnose motivation/ability, rank student worth, assign programme, create an official study plan, submit applications, or override the SYV.

## Workflow integration stance

MVP uses share link and accessible print/PDF-like export. It does **not** write to school information systems, Beda, admissions systems, journal/record systems or official individual study plans. Integration would require separate necessity, legal, security and vendor analysis. Beda retrieval is restricted to listed admission/administrative purposes and is not a general consumer profile API. [S26](SOURCES.md#product-education-and-data-sources)

## SYV pilot tasks

1. Review 12 synthetic briefs, including not-yet-eligible, conflict, missing data and non-traditional choice cases.
2. Find the authority, date and rule trace for a claim.
3. Identify where AI was used.
4. Prepare for and conduct a student session using a brief.
5. Submit a correction.
6. Suggest a next action and observe student acceptance flow.
7. Attempt inappropriate access/export in a role test.

## Success hypotheses

- Median preparation time does not increase; qualitative conversation quality improves.
- At least 90% of critical synthetic cases are judged safe and correctly bounded by two independent SYVs.
- Students report the brief represents their own words and preserves agency.
- No SYV feels encouraged to treat the brief as an official assessment.
- Correction flow produces actionable reports without an unmanageable moderation burden.

These are pass criteria to test, not current outcomes.
